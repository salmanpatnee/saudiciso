# Data Model: Industry Management

## Industry Entity

### Fields
- **industry_id** (integer, primary key, auto-increment)
  - Unique identifier for each industry
  - Auto-generated by the database
- **industry_name** (string, required, unique)
  - The name of the industry
  - Must be unique across all industries
  - Maximum length: 255 characters
- **sector** (string, nullable)
  - The sector the industry belongs to (optional)
  - Maximum length: 255 characters

### Relationships
- No direct relationships specified in requirements
- May be referenced by other entities in the system (e.g., companies, employees)

### Validation Rules
- industry_name:
  - Required field
  - Unique constraint (case-insensitive)
  - Maximum length: 255 characters
  - Cannot be empty or contain only whitespace
- sector:
  - Optional field
  - Maximum length: 255 characters
  - If provided, cannot be empty or contain only whitespace

### Business Rules
- Industry names must be unique within the system
- Industry records should not be deleted if they are referenced by other entities (soft delete consideration)
- Industry names should support standard alphanumeric characters, spaces, and common punctuation

## Access Patterns
- List all industries (with pagination)
- Get specific industry by ID
- Create new industry
- Update existing industry
- Delete industry (with validation to prevent deletion of referenced records)
- Search/filter industries by name or sector
